## How each one was measured

### Line matching

- **Bar:** 92 per cent or more correct at the first attempt
- **Measured:** 99.6 per cent
- **Sample:** 4108 clear lines across 200 requests
- Correct 4,090 of 4,108; 18 clear line(s) were flagged instead of matched.

### Ambiguity handling

- **Bar:** 95 per cent or more of genuinely unclear lines flagged, and no more than 3 per cent of clear lines wrongly flagged
- **Measured:** 100.0 per cent of unclear lines flagged; 0.4 per cent of clear lines wrongly flagged
- **Sample:** 892 unclear lines and 4108 clear lines
- Flagged 892 of 892 genuinely unclear lines; 18 of 4,108 clear lines were flagged when they should not have been.

### Pricing integrity

- **Bar:** every price on a quote equals the pricing engine's own output
- **Measured:** 23 of 23 priced quotes match the engine
- **Sample:** 23 priced quote version(s)
- **Note:** The server stores a fingerprint of every figure the engine produced. Re-running the engine has to reproduce it exactly, or the quote cannot be approved or sent.

### Revisions

- **Bar:** every change between two versions detected
- **Measured:** 100.0 per cent of changes detected
- **Sample:** 30 pairs of requests
- 144 of 144 changes detected.

### Speed

- **Bar:** a draft ready within 15 minutes at the ninetieth percentile, for 40 lines
- **Measured:** 1.35 seconds at the ninetieth percentile
- **Sample:** 200 requests, scaled to 40 lines each
- **Note:** This is the agent's own working time with the built-in engine. It excludes time waiting for a customer or a supplier, and it excludes network time when an external model is active.
- Median 0.78 s, slowest 4.082 s, scaled to forty lines.

### Edits by the salesperson

- **Bar:** a median of two lines or fewer changed between the draft and the sent quote
- **Measured:** not measured
- **Sample:** none
- **Note:** This bar can only be measured once salespeople are editing real drafts. The product records the draft and the sent version of every quote, so the figure becomes measurable from the first week of a pilot. It is reported as not measured rather than estimated.

## What this report does not tell you

- **Edits by the salesperson** cannot be measured without salespeople
  editing real drafts. The product records the draft and the sent
  version of every quote, so the figure becomes available in the first
  week of a pilot. It is reported as not measured rather than estimated.
- **Speed** is the agent's own working time. It excludes waiting for a
  customer or a supplier, which is the part that actually takes days,
  and with the built-in engine it excludes network time.
- **The test set is generated** from the same catalogue the product
  searches. It exercises the matching rules hard, including lines built
  with an attribute deliberately removed or contradicted, but it is not
  a sample of one customer's real traffic. Replacing it with real
  anonymised requests is the first thing discovery should do.
